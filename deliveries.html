Delivery Information

If you've arranged a delivery through our local shop, we’ll keep you informed every step of the way.

  • 🕒 Delivery date and time will be arranged in-store when the item is purchased. You will be sent a text and email confirming the details, along with a reference number.
  • 📧 You can use this reference number to check the progress of your delivery in the "Delivery Status" section of this page.
  • 🚚 We will not be able to construct or assemble items on delivery.
  • 🏠 Very large items will be delivered to the ground floor only.
  • 💸 Delivery pricing will be based on the distance from our shop to your delivery address.
  • 📞 On the day of delivery, you will receive an update via text/email when the van driver is en route. This will also be updated in the progress checker.
