Collection Information

If you wish to donate items to Shaw Trust, please read through the following important information.

  • ⚠️ We cannot guarantee that we will accept all items for donation. We assess donations based on their condition.
  • 💺 Chairs, sofas, rugs, and similar items must still have fire safety labels attached. This is required for all items sold with a fire label.
  • 📞 We will contact you to arrange a collection date and time via your preferred contact method (call, text, or email). You can choose more than one contact method when registering your donation.
